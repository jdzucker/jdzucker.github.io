About

An engineer from ISAE-SUPAERO and PhD in Machine Learning (Sorbonne University, 1996), Jean-Daniel Zucker is Senior Research Director of Exceptional Class (DRCE) at IRD and Deputy Director of UMI UMMISCO since January 2025 (Director 2014–2024). He is Professor of Computer Science at Sorbonne University and Paris Dauphine (PSL), and co-head of the Integromics group.

His research focuses on AI and supervised and unsupervised Machine Learning for complex systems modeling and medical decision support. Application domains include metagenomics, nutrigenomics, epidemiology, and environmental sciences.
